# Terms of Service

**CEDA Innovation Management Portal**
Version 1.0 (draft) · Last updated 14 November 2026

> **Prototype notice:** This portal is a prototype built for the CEDA Innovation Management Portal Hackathon. These terms are a draft for demonstration. CEDA must review them (including its legal and data protection teams) before any live use.

## 1. About these terms

The Citizen Entrepreneurial Development Agency ("CEDA", "we", "us") runs the CEDA Innovation Management Portal (the "Portal"). Through the Portal, people in Botswana submit innovative ideas, receive feedback and follow their progress through review, approval and award.

By creating an account or using the Portal, you agree to these Terms and to our Privacy Notice. If you do not agree, please do not use the Portal.

## 2. Who can use the Portal

- You must be 18 years or older.
- Individuals must be citizens of Botswana. Groups and businesses must be citizen-owned entities, as confirmed in the declaration on the submission form. A specific call may set additional eligibility rules.
- CEDA staff, evaluators, adjudicators, administrators and committee members receive access from CEDA and must also follow CEDA's confidentiality rules.

## 3. Your account

- Give accurate information and keep it up to date.
- Confirm your email address using the verification link we send you.
- Keep your password private. Do not share your account with anyone.
- Tell us quickly if you think someone else has used your account.
- You are responsible for activity on your account until you tell us about a problem.
- We may suspend or deactivate an account that breaks these Terms or puts the Portal or other users at risk.

## 4. Submitting ideas

**4.1 Accuracy.** Information you give must be true and complete. False or misleading information can lead to rejection, withdrawal of an award or other action.

**4.2 Originality and rights.** Submit only ideas that are yours, or that you have the right to submit. Do not infringe anyone else's intellectual property. Make sure team members you list agree to be included.

**4.3 Ownership.** You keep ownership of your idea and its intellectual property. Submitting an idea does not transfer ownership to CEDA.

**4.4 Permission for CEDA.** You give CEDA a non-exclusive permission to store, copy, view and share your submission within the Portal with authorised reviewers. CEDA uses it only to assess your idea, run the programme, produce programme statistics and comply with the law.

**4.5 Confidentiality.** Only assigned CEDA staff, evaluators, adjudicators and committee members who are bound by confidentiality can see your submission. CEDA will not publish your idea without your permission. Evaluator identities and individual scores are not shown to submitters.

**4.6 Documents.** Upload only documents you are allowed to share. Do not upload files that contain viruses or harmful code. The accepted formats and size limits are shown on the form.

**4.7 Editing and resubmitting.** You can edit a draft at any time. After you submit, you cannot edit the idea unless evaluators ask for changes. You can then respond and resubmit within the deadline given.

## 5. Evaluation, feedback and decisions

- Ideas move through these stages: submission, review, feedback, approval and award.
- Timelines shown in the Portal (for example a typical 14-day turnaround) are targets, not guarantees.
- Evaluators and adjudicators score ideas against criteria set by CEDA. Decisions are made by CEDA's committees on that basis.
- Submitting an idea, or receiving a positive review or approval, does not guarantee funding, an award or any other support. Awards are subject to CEDA's funding rules and any agreement you sign.
- If you disagree with a decision, you may use CEDA's appeals and complaints procedures, available from the Help Center.

## 6. Acceptable use

You must not:

- break the law or the rights of others;
- upload harmful, offensive, fraudulent or misleading content;
- try to access another person's account or data, or bypass security;
- overload or disrupt the Portal, or use automated tools to submit or collect data;
- pretend to be someone else; or
- harass CEDA staff or other users, including in live chat.

## 7. Live chat and notifications

Live chat is available from 09:00 to 16:00 (Central Africa Time) on working days. Outside these hours you can leave a message. We send notifications about your submissions in the Portal and by email, and they are part of the service.

## 8. Availability and changes

We aim to keep the Portal available 24 hours a day, but we cannot promise uninterrupted service. Maintenance, outages and events outside our control can happen. We may change or remove features.
