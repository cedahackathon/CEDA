# Privacy Notice

**CEDA Innovation Management Portal**
Version 1.0 (draft) · Last updated 14 November 2026

> **Prototype notice:** This portal is a prototype built for the CEDA Innovation Management Portal Hackathon. This notice is a draft for demonstration. CEDA's data protection team must review it before any live use.

## 1. Who we are

The Citizen Entrepreneurial Development Agency ("CEDA", "we", "us") is responsible for your personal data in the CEDA Innovation Management Portal (the "Portal"). We handle it in line with the Data Protection Act, 2024 (Act No. 18 of 2024) of Botswana.

## 2. What we collect

**Account and identity details**
- Name, email address, mobile number and password (stored securely by our authentication provider, never in plain text).
- Omang or passport number, date of birth and gender (optional), used to confirm who you are and whether you are eligible.
- District, town or village and postal or physical address.

**Business details (if you apply as a business or group)**
- Trading name, CIPA registration number and BURS tax identification number.
- Names, roles and phone numbers of team members you add. Please make sure they know you are sharing their details.

**Your submission**
- The text of your idea, funding request, supporting documents and any responses to feedback.

**Evaluation records**
- Scores, comments, feedback and decisions about your idea.

**Technical and security information**
- Sign-in times, IP address, device and browser type, and a record of important actions in the Portal (an audit trail).

**Messages**
- Live chat messages and notifications.

## 3. Why we use your data

- **To run the service:** create your account, receive and assess your idea, give feedback and record decisions and awards.
- **To confirm eligibility and prevent fraud:** check identity and entity details.
- **To keep the Portal secure:** detect misuse, protect accounts and keep an audit trail.
- **To communicate with you:** send status updates, feedback requests and support replies.
- **To report on the programme:** produce statistics and reports that do not identify you, unless you agree.
- **To meet legal and audit requirements.**

We rely on your consent (for example when you accept the Terms and this Notice), on what is needed to deal with your submission, on our legal obligations, and on CEDA's legitimate interests and public-interest functions in running enterprise development programmes.

## 4. Who can see your information

- **CEDA staff, evaluators, adjudicators and committee members** (internal and external) who need it for their role. They are bound by confidentiality, and each role only sees what it needs. Evaluators do not see your personal details at the screening stage, and committee members see progress information without scores or personal details.
- **Our service providers**, such as cloud hosting, authentication and email delivery providers, who process data for us under our instructions.
- **Authorities**, where the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Firebase (Google Cloud) servers in [HOSTING_REGION]. If data is processed outside Botswana, we take steps to make sure it is protected as the law requires.

## 6. How long we keep it (prototype defaults)

- **Draft ideas:** deleted after 12 months without activity.
- **Submitted ideas, evaluations and decisions:** kept while we run and report on the programme and meet funding and audit requirements. We expect this to be about 5 years, to be confirmed by CEDA.
- **Account details:** kept while your account is active, then deleted or anonymised unless we must keep them for the records above.
- **Audit logs and security records:** kept for about 3 years.
- **Chat messages:** kept for about 12 months.

## 7. How we protect it

- Encrypted connections between your device and the Portal.
- Role-based access, so people only see what their role allows.
- Masking of sensitive details such as your Omang number on screen.
- An audit trail of important actions.
- Email verification and password protection for accounts.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell the Information and Data Protection Commission and, where required, you, as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

You have the right to:

- be told how your data is used (this Notice);
- access the data we hold about you;
- have inaccurate data corrected;
- ask for your data to be deleted;
- ask us to limit how we use it, or object to certain uses;
- receive your data in a usable format;
- withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect past use); and
- complain to the Information and Data Protection Commission.

Some rights are limited where we must keep records by law, or where an idea is already part of an evaluation. To use any right, contact us using the details below. We will respond within the time the law allows.

## 9. Children

The Portal is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 10. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what the Portal needs to work: a sign-in session and your language preference. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## 11. Changes to this notice

We may update this Notice. The version and date at the top will change. For important changes we will tell you in the Portal or by email.
